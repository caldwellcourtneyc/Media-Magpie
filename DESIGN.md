# Design canon

The look is settled. This file is the reference for any new screen.

## Structure

- **Desktop (1180px and up): three columns.** Left nav (brand, search, media types with counts, views, settings and the light/dark switch), main column, and the right rail, which is **Add something** on every page: the + sheet laid open, 360px wide, with the same four tabs and forms. It folds to a slim strip with a + and remembers open or folded on each device. It starts on the page's type (a Books filter opens it on Book) and, on Shiny things, on the capture tab. Nothing in it takes focus until you pick a tab, so loading a page never pops a keyboard. A line typed into its Shiny things tab survives a reload: the rail reopens on that tab until it's saved.
- **Mid width (860 to 1179px):** no room for the rail; the nav gains an Add something button that opens the + sheet.
- **Phone (under 860px):** brand bar on top with the search box, the light/dark switch and Settings (a cog), a sticky row of type chips under it (it scrolls sideways, and brings the lit chip into view on load and after a pick, so Books, Podcasts or Music never sit picked off the edge), and a bottom tab bar: Now, Library, **+**, Up next, Shiny things. The **+** is in the middle of the bar on every screen, so adding is always one tap. Sheets rise from the bottom.
- **A type filter filters the page you're on,** on every page (decided 2026-10-28: jumping to another page felt unintuitive).
- **Test copy:** with `TEST_COPY=1` (a laptop copy, when a hosted copy is the real one) a thin ink strip across the top of every page, the sign-in page included, reads "Test copy: not your real library".
- **Keyboard:** the first Tab offers "Skip to the page"; a sheet takes focus when it opens and hands it back to what opened it.

## Views

- **Now (home).** Built for deciding what to watch, with what you're reading alongside. **Watching** shows every show or movie in progress as big covers, each saying what lands next when TVmaze has a date ("New episode Oct 21", "Finale Oct 21", "Season 2 starts Nov 6") and where you are once you've said (no prompt when you haven't). On desk and mid width the covers keep one size (about 140px wide) and fill the row, so a wide screen gets more to a row instead of bigger covers and Up Next stays in view (picked from mockups, 2026-11-15); the phone keeps them three across. **Reading** sits directly under Watching (picked from mockups, 2026-11-15: Now was watch-only, and a book on the go was two taps away): books whose queue is In Progress, as covers the size of Watching's, each with your progress text once you've set one, in your own order; it isn't drawn when no book is in progress. **Coming soon** (next 30 days) sits under them and above Up Next: the next 30 days of Coming soon, minus things you're watching (their covers already say it) and anything No Access, up to five dated lines (three across on desk and mid width, one column on the phone) with a link to the full list. It isn't drawn at all when nothing is close. **Up Next** follows: TV and movies in your Up Next queue, minus anything marked No Access, with a quiet link to those ("2 more are waiting until they're on a service you have"). Adding lives in the right rail (the + on the phone) and captures live in Shiny things, so neither is on Now. Now and Up next share the **who's-watching chips** and **your own order** (below). **Books picked on Now** shows that Reading section alone, with a quiet link under it to the books in Up Next and Saved for Later ("7 more books are waiting in Up next", "1 more book is waiting in Up next", no link at none); with no book on the go it's one line ("No book on the go. Your reading lives in Up next.") and a button there. Podcasts and music are not on home: picking one of them on Now stays on Now and shows one line saying where they live ("Podcasts live in the Library") with a button there. Finished items have no timeline view; their history lives on each item's page.
- **First run (an empty library).** A fresh copy starts empty (picked from mockups, 2026-11-15: a fictional demo with no covers was a poor first look). While the library has no items, Now is a welcome instead: the heading "Your library is empty.", one line under it, and three first steps that open the Add form on the right tab, in the rail on desk (unfolding it) and the + sheet elsewhere: **Add a show** (Queue, TV, In Progress picked), **Queue something** (Queue) and **Capture a line** (Shiny things). A text link, "Or load a small demo library to look around", loads the fictional demo as ordinary items; the server refuses it once the library has anything live. Under them sits the **ghost shelf**: one row of about ten well-known titles' covers with no heading of its own, faint, with the line "These are examples. Your first add replaces them." above it at full strength. The titles are one plain list (`lib/examples.ts`); the repo holds no cover images or links. Covers are looked up after the page is up, a few at a time, and kept for the session; while loading, or when a lookup finds nothing or fails, a title shows the usual missing-cover tile. The examples are aria-hidden and inert, are never saved, and never appear in counts, search or exports. The welcome and the shelf go with the first item (and return only if the library is emptied again).
- **Sign-in.** The stacked lockup, the tagline, the password box. On a copy using the embedded database, one quiet line under the form says where the password is ("Running it on your own computer? The password is in .env.local, where the first run wrote it."), never what it is; a hosted database doesn't show it.
- **Library.** Everything you have, under **Top Shelf, Faves, Loves, No tier yet, Not a Fan.** Nothing defaults to Love: something you haven't rated (an unread book in the queue, say) sits under No tier yet. **Not a Fan** always sits at the very bottom as its own section with Fold (remembered on that device). Podcasts and music live here, since they are followed rather than logged per listen. Rows name a status only when it isn't the norm (Paused, Dropped, Ended, Watching, Caught up).
  - **Everything opens By type: five rooms.** One band per type, in your order of types (TV, Movies, Music, Podcasts, Books until you change it in Settings), with its count and an Open link (the room opens at its top), each a cover shelf that scrolls sideways and holds every item, Top Shelf and Faves first and marked on the cover.
  - **A type opens as its own room, by Tier.** The type's name and swatch head the page. Top Shelf and Faves appear once they hold something (until then one line says how to fill them); covers get smaller as the tier drops (Top Shelf only a step above Faves), and Top Shelf has no colored edge. Loves, then No tier yet, split by your own lists where you have them (podcasts, books), biggest first and "Not on a list" last; music splits into albums and soundtracks (first, there are few) and artists. TV is one grid with the network under each cover.
  - **Your lists:** dense rows under your own list names, loud headings, jump chips to each. Rows carry a small cover (square for podcasts and music), title, person, capture count, and a tier badge only for Fave and Top Shelf (Love is the norm here, so it stays quiet).
  - **By author** (books only): the same rows under each author, filed by surname.
  - **A to Z:** the same rows under letters.
- **Search.** One box in the nav (phone: top bar) searches everything from any screen: titles, people, list names, finish notes and captures. Results replace the main column, grouped by type under loud headings with each type's swatch, then matching captures. A row says why it matched when it wasn't the title. Escape or changing view clears it.
- **Coming soon** on Up next is compact dated lines, the same line Now uses and no covers (picked from mockups, 2026-11-15: the row of covers took a screen to say three dates): every new season or episode with a date from TV shows in the library, and every movie with a release date still ahead, soonest first, whether or not they're queued and whether or not you're watching them. Three lines across on desk and mid width, one to a row on the phone. Each leads with the date in the display face ("Today", "Tomorrow", "Oct 21"), then the title and what's happening ("Season 2 starts", "Season 6 finale", "Season 2 airing · finale Nov 25"); a line opens the item. Everything in the library counts as liked unless it's Not a Fan or its status is Dropped (Paused stays), so a liked show from your history shows up too; both are left out on Now as well. **Nothing undated ever appears,** and with nothing dated the section isn't drawn at all, as on Now. An entry leaves by itself once its date passes and TVmaze has nothing next. **Hide lives on the item's own page,** not on the line: About's Coming soon row has Hide from Coming Soon (with Undo) and, once hidden, Show again. Hiding takes one season out and the next season brings the show back; a movie stays out until shown again. On Up next, "Show N hidden" under the lines lists the hidden ones dimmed, each with Show again (and the heading stays, with that link, when the only dated things are hidden). Sections fold with Fold, so Hide only ever means this. Show dates come from TVmaze through the server, cached on each show and refreshed about twice a day (ended shows weekly). A movie's release date ("Comes out Nov 6") is set in Edit details or by a script (a recommendations run, say) from a source it read, never guessed (decided 2026-10-28, in place of a TMDB key).
- **Up next.** **In Progress** first (it reads Watching under TV or Movies, Reading under Books and Listening under podcasts or music), then **Coming soon**, **Up Next**, **Saved for Later**, and **No Access** at the very bottom with Fold: everything queued that isn't on your services, whichever section it's in. No descriptions under the headings. Every section but Coming soon is cover tiles, newest first unless you moved them; under each cover, where you are once you've said (no prompt when you haven't), or where it streams. A tile opens the item, where finishing, following and moving live. A service filter row appears once anything is on a service (your pick or the TVmaze default, see the item sheet); filtering to a service leaves Watching out, so Coming soon leads, and leaves out any section with nothing in it; filtering to No Access shows those items in their own sections.
- **Who's-watching chips** (decided 2026-11-03; only when `WATCH_WITH_LABEL` is set, so nobody's name is in the code). One row at the top of Now and Up next, above the service filter: Everything, Solo, and the label. They narrow the Watching, Up Next, Saved, No Access and Coming soon lists and carry from one page to the other; the choice sits in the URL, so Back and a reload keep it and a fresh open starts on Everything. Solo is anything not tagged, so nothing needs sorting first. Only for what you watch: picking Books, Podcasts or Music hides the row and ignores the choice; under Everything, Solo includes them and the label leaves them out. An item's **Who with** row (TV and movies, above Tier) sets it, and the tag goes last in `tags`, so a first tag stays your list. TV and movie rooms stay one grid; the label shows as a list only under Your lists.
- **Your own order** (decided 2026-11-03). Each queue section has one order, shared by every filter: newest on top unless you moved it, and something newly queued still lands on top. With a mouse, drag a cover beside another in the same list; a bar shows where it lands (no dragging on touch, where a press has to stay a tap or a scroll, nor in No Access, which holds several sections). Everywhere, an item's **Order** row (under Queue) says "3 of 13" and has Move to top, Move up and Move down (on a show being watched it reads "Order in Watching", since that show has no Queue row). Up and down step past what's beside it in the list it was opened from, so the move always shows there; Move to top goes above everything in the section, filtered out or not. Changing section starts it fresh on top.
- **Item sheet.** Docked right on desk, a bottom sheet on the phone. Its header is the cover on the sheet's own ground (no blurred backdrop) beside the type tag, title, creator and year (no year on a podcast: the lookup's year is its newest episode's, which says nothing about the show; the add form's results and the covers review leave it out too), where you stand, the next episode or season when one is dated, and Edit details or cover. Under the header, tabs: **About** (status, where to watch, tier, queue, where it came from, Delete), **Captures** and **History**, each with its count. Podcasts, artists and albums have nothing to log, so they get About and Captures, plus History only when imported finishes exist. Every tab stays mounted while the sheet is open, so a half-typed finish survives a tab switch. Section labels inside are loud. **About leads with what's already there** (picked from mockups, 2026-11-15: About was only controls, with the latest finish and captures a tab away). **Last time**, once it has a finish: the newest one's date ("No date" when undated), its detail or Rewatch label and its note, worded as History words them but with no Edit or Delete, then the log-again button ("Log a rewatch", opening History's form) and, with more than one finish, a text link to History ("All 2 in History"). **Shiny things**, once it has a capture: the newest one as the Captures tab draws it (a tap opens it), and with more than one a link to Captures ("All 3 in Captures"). Tier comes right under them, then the other rows in their usual order. With no finish and no capture, About is the controls alone. **Find in Libby** (books and authors, once a library is added in Settings) is a row on About, one chip per library, each a Libby search in a new tab; it sits where a show's where to watch does, after the lead blocks and Tier (on an author, under Status), not in the header, where the links wrapped badly on the phone (picked 2026-11-15). What an item is decides what About offers:
  - **Followed** (podcasts, music artists, authors): a Status control (Following, Paused, Dropped, plus Ended for podcasts, meaning they stopped making it), then tier. The Captures tab offers "+ Capture from an episode". No finishes to log, so About leads with Shiny things only (and Last time, with no button, when imported finishes exist).
  - **Albums:** tier only on About. Nobody logs a listen.
  - **TV:** "Where you are" (Watching, Caught up and waiting, Done) with **seasons seen** as chips right under it, the one record of how far you are: you mark a season when you finish it, not each episode, and covers say "Through S2" (nothing when you haven't marked any). Once every season out is marked, a button offers Caught up (Done if TVmaze says the show has ended); it never switches by itself. Shows have no free-text progress box and no "Log a season" form, and History appears only on a show that already has earlier entries (decided 2026-10-28). Then a Coming soon line when the next episode or season has a date (with Hide from Coming Soon, or Show again), where to watch on every show (not only queued ones), then tier and queue. **A show being watched has no Queue row** (caught in mockups, 2026-11-15: Where you are said Watching while the Queue row under it lit "Not queued" beside an order of "5 of 5"). Where you are covers it, and the Order row reads "Order in Watching". The Queue row (Not queued, Up Next, Saved for Later) is back as soon as Where you are isn't Watching, so a watched show goes to Up Next or Saved for Later by changing Where you are first. **Where to watch defaults to the TVmaze network** when that network is one of your services ("Apple TV" is Apple, "HBO Max" is HBO; a channel that streams elsewhere, like FX, doesn't count). The default is shown, never stored: its chip is lit with "From TVmaze. Pick another to change it." under the row; picking a service replaces it, tapping the lit default keeps it as yours, and clearing your pick falls back to it. Tiles, filters and Coming soon use the same default. A show's earlier History entries are never labelled a rewatch: the detail says what each was. A show with earlier entries shows the newest under Last time with no log button, as its History tab has none.
  - **Movies and books:** a movie with a release date ahead shows a Coming soon line first (with Hide from Coming Soon); then tier and queue; History logs each finish. Edit details has a Release date on movies, and calls the creator field Network on shows. A queued one also has **Log it as read/watched** on About, under the queue chips, opening History's form there (asked for 2026-10-27: finishing is the usual reason to open a queued thing, and History was too far away to find). Once it has a finish, Last time's button is that button, so it never shows twice. **The finish form asks the tier too** (picked from mockups, 2026-11-15: things finished from the queue were piling up under No tier yet): under the date, detail and note, a row labelled "Tier (optional)" with the same four chips, lit as the item stands when the form opens (a rewatch of a Top Shelf film shows Top Shelf lit, an unrated one lights nothing). Log it saves the tier with the finish only if you changed it; left alone, no tier is written, and Cancel changes nothing. Editing an old finish has no tier row. A queued movie's where to watch adds **Theater** after the services (asked for 2026-10-26: ones to see on the big screen), so the Up next filter row doubles as a theater list. TV never shows it.
  - **Tier row everywhere** (item sheet and the + sheet): Not a Fan, Love, Fave, Top Shelf. Nothing is lit until you pick one, and tapping the lit one clears it; Not a Fan and a tier exclude each other.
- **+ sheet** (the phone's +, the nav's Add something button at mid width, "n" on a keyboard, and the rail's form on desk): Queue (anything), Finished (TV, movies, books), Follow (podcasts, artists, authors, shows between seasons), Shiny things (a capture). It opens on the last tab used, Queue the first time, and on Shiny things when opened from the Shiny things page. On the phone it keeps one height across its tabs, so the tab row stays put. A capture attaches to something by searching your log (no quick-pick chips, nothing prefilled); attached, it has an optional episode, track or page. The title box reads "Search TV titles, or type your own" (the type's word in lower case, TV in capitals). Under "In your log", each match says what picking it does as a small ink pill in sentence case (Queue it, Follow it, Log it, or Log again once it has a finish), so it reads as the action and not a label.
- **Capture form** (the + sheet's Shiny things tab, the rail's, and an opened capture). The kinds read **Note, Idea, Link, Quote** (here and in Shiny things' filter), and a new capture starts on Note. Fields: kind, the words, an image, From (a search of your log; the standalone where, who and type wait behind one text link under it, "Not in your log? Say where it's from", which opens them in place, and they are already open whenever one of them holds something, so nothing is ever hidden or dropped), episode or page and, for podcasts, TV, movies and music, **Time** (type `1309`, it reads `13:09` once you leave the box), who said it, link, note, date, and the **Talk about it** switch (a chip, lit when on). After a talk, an opened capture shows "Talked about Oct 20" and Claude's one line in a quiet block of its own above the switch, never inside your note. **Save stays in reach** (picked 2026-11-15): the Save button is pinned to the bottom of the sheet or the rail, on that surface's own ground and clear of the phone's home bar, while the form scrolls under it; scrolled to the end it sits after the last field and covers nothing.
  - **A Note or an Idea is its own words,** so it has no second "Your note" box (one shows if a note is already saved). Dates read like the rest of the app ("Keep Oct 27").
  - **The phone's form is the quick one:** kind, the image button (so a screenshot-only mark needs no scrolling), the words, From, episode and time, then Talk about it (order decided 2026-11-15); who said it, link, note and date fold under "More: ...". The cursor starts in the words (decided 2026-11-15). Desk and mid width keep the words first, every field open.
  - **Marks:** the words are optional. With none, the button reads **Save to finish later**, and the capture is kept as a mark if it has an image or says where it's from. An opened mark is titled **Finish this**; its screenshot leads, full width but no taller than 40% of the screen (its top showing, where the episode and time usually are), and a tap opens it whole on a dark ground in place (picked 2026-10-28, so you can read it while you write). The words box says "Write it up".
  - **Images:** an "Upload Image?" button (no label above it) picks one picture per capture (on the phone, the photo library); with one picked it reads "Change image". It's shrunk on the device to a JPEG of a few hundred KB (long side up to 1800px, so a lock screen's small print stays readable) before upload, shown as a small thumbnail (52 by 72, the size of a mark's on Shiny things), with Remove image beside it; a saved one opens full size in a new tab (on Finish this, in place, as above). Picking only a screenshot makes a mark. **Pasting works too** (asked for 2026-11-15): a paste anywhere in the form, when the clipboard holds an image, attaches it exactly as if picked (text pasted into a box stays an ordinary paste), and a **Paste image** button beside Upload reads the clipboard where the browser allows it, with a line under it, "No image on the clipboard", when there is none or the browser says no (a line, not a toast, which would sit on Save).
- **Shiny things.** Every capture: notes, ideas, links and quotes in two columns. The header's + Capture button shows at mid width and on the phone; on desk the rail beside it is already the open capture form, so it isn't drawn. Chips: All kinds, the four kinds, then **To finish**, **To talk about** and **Talked about** (decided 2026-10-20).
  - **Marks** sit in the main list like any capture, "To finish" in the label line, with the screenshot as a thumbnail, where it's from ("Where from?" when nothing says), then episode and time. A mark opens to be written up (not the thing it's from). The To finish chip lists only marks.
  - **Talked about:** once a capture has been talked through (and isn't switched back on), it leaves the main list and the kind chips and shows only under the Talked about chip, with its date and "Where we landed: ..." (Claude's line). Switching Talk about it back on brings it back.
  - **Written by Claude:** when Claude finishes a mark without you agreeing the words, the capture carries "Written by Claude" in its label line and above the words when opened. Rewriting the words in the app makes them yours and drops the tag.
  - A finished capture with a screenshot keeps a small thumbnail of it (40 by 56, top right), so it's plain the image is still there. A saved link is a real link, opening in a new tab (only http and https links are ever stored, from the forms or an import).
  - Finished tiles don't show the time; the opened capture does, and so does an item's Captures tab ("Ep. 9 · at 13:09"), where a mark reads "To finish: ...". "Capture" stays the word for the act and for one entry (the sheet's title, toasts, the item sheet's Captures tab); Shiny things names the place they live and the tab that adds one.
- Every view filters by media type.
- **Settings.** Three loud group headings, each over an ink rule (picked 2026-11-15, in place of seven sections in one column at one weight). **Library:** Your data (counts, Export, Import), Order of types, Covers, Recently deleted. **This device:** Light or dark, Sign out. **Connections:** Libby, then **For developers** folded shut (the database, where backups go and how long they're kept, and the scripts API: paths, env names and endpoints read like errors to fix if they sit open). On desk (1180px and up) two columns, Library on the left, This device and Connections stacked on the right; below that one column in that order. A section says itself in one line, and Light or dark and Sign out need none. The backup line is "Backed up automatically, one file a day."; on a hosted database it says backups are files pulled to a computer by a scheduled task. Libby keeps one line by its add form on where a library's code is found. A deleted item with a live copy of the same title says so, and its button reads Restore anyway.
- **Order of types** (picked from mockups, 2026-11-15). The five types are listed TV, Movies, Music, Podcasts, Books until you reorder them in Settings: five rows, each the type's swatch and name with Move up and Move down chips (the first row's Move up and the last row's Move down off), the queue's Order pattern, so touch works and nothing drags. A move shows at once and saves; a failed save says so and puts the saved order back. It sits under Library, not This device, because it is kept on the server and holds on every device. The order applies everywhere the types are listed: the desk nav and the phone's chip bar (Everything stays first), the Library's By type rooms, the Add form's type chips (+ sheet and rail, each tab showing its own types in that order), the capture form's standalone type chips, Search's type groups and the covers review. Validation, exports and the API keep the fixed order.

## Color: one job each

- Five hues mean media type, from the Flower Market palette: cyan TV `#01b2d4`, orange movies `#ee5802`, magenta music `#df3cb5`, green podcasts `#629e46`, violet books `#6b52d0` (one shade brighter than the board's `#5d45be`, so it clears 3:1 on navy). Gold `#fbd101` means Top Shelf. **One set in both modes**, the same values on dark and light.
- **Text is never set in a hue, and never sits on a hue fill.** White on lime or teal is unreadable, and the rule is white text on saturated fields, so no field gets text. Selected chips, type chips included, use ink inversion with the hue as a swatch inside; type tags are a tint of their hue with ink text.
- Nothing else gets a hue. Buttons, selected chips and toasts use ink inversion (light ink on dark, dark ink on light). Fave and Love badges are neutral with gold strength rising by tier.
- Dark mode is dark navy (ground `#040f22`, panel `#081a33`) in a staged radial gradient, never flat. B's structure and neutrals, on navy.
- Light mode uses D's tokens: cool gray ground and white main panel.
- **Missing covers:** in light mode an ink tile with a white title and a band of the type color along the bottom (a pale tint read as washed out); in dark mode a tint of the type color with ink text. The title never sits on the hue. At the covers review's thumbnail size (56px wide) it's the band and the type label alone: a title breaks mid-word there, and it's beside it in the row.

Exact values are the tokens at the top of `app/globals.css`.

## Brand

- **Mark:** a magpie on the right rim of a woven nest holding five cards in the media hues (TV, movies, music, podcasts, books, left to right). The nest and bird take the current ink (dark on light, white on navy); the cards keep their hues. Source: `components/Logo.tsx` (generated `components/logoMark.ts`).
- **Wordmark:** Jost, thin (200) "Media" over heavy (900) "Magpie". Used only in the lockup.
- **Lockups:** stacked (mark above the words, centered on the nest, the bird hanging to the side) is the primary one: the login page and anything standalone. **Tagline:** "shiny things for eyes and ears" (lowercase, no period), centered under the stacked lockup on the sign-in page. Horizontal (the two-line wordmark with the mark on its right, so the bird sits on the outer edge; bottoms aligned, the nest on the "Magpie" baseline; a tight gap) where space is short: the nav and the phone bar. Clicking either in the app goes to Now.
- **Small sizes:** at 32px the bird drops out (cards and nest); at 16px it's three cards (TV, music, books) over a bowl. Icons: `public/icon.svg` (flips to white in dark browsers), `public/favicon-32.png`, `public/apple-icon.png` (white mark on navy).

## Type

One pairing in both modes: **Schibsted Grotesk** for display (headings, day numbers, stats), **Figtree** for body; the wordmark alone uses Jost (see Brand). Self-hosted through `next/font`. Body sizes come from one scale (`--fs-tiny` 12, `--fs-meta` 13, `--fs-card` 15, `--fs-body` 16, `--fs-lead` 17); use a step, don't add a size.

## Rules for new screens

- Look like B/D, not like a component library: rows and typographic structure over boxed cards.
- No shadowed chunky cards with per-card accent bars. The thin colored rule on a quote is the blockquote convention, kept deliberately; nothing else gets one.
- No cream ground. No em dashes in UI copy.
- Section and nav labels are loud (display face, heavy weight), never small gray metadata.
- Motion only where it reveals: covers lift on hover, sheets slide in. Respect reduced motion.
- Dates stay out of the way. A finish is dated today without asking ("Finished today · Change"); editing an old one shows its date. Captures keep their date controls, since dates matter more there.
- Inputs are 16px so phones do not zoom. Touch targets are at least 36px, primary actions 48px; on the phone, text links get a 44px tap height though they read as text.

## Where it came from

The B (dark) and D (light) directions from the round-2 prototype, picked as one design with a toggle. The prototype file is not part of the repo.
